The reason most TUIs look like developer prototypes is that they're built feature-first. Someone needs a list, they reach for List. They need a dialog, they reach for Popup. Each widget arrives with its own assumptions about borders, padding, color, and none of them know about each other. The result looks assembled, not designed.
